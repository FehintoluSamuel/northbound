Questions

Before you enroll

Do I need prior trading experience?

No. The curriculum begins with market fundamentals and develops from there. No stock-market background is assumed.

When are the live sessions?

Live sessions take place on Thursday, Saturday, and Sunday. Each session lasts two hours.

How much time should I set aside?

Plan for about two hours of study on each weekday, in addition to the live sessions.

How do I get enrollment and pricing information?

Apply through the main application. Enrollment and pricing details are provided there.

What will I learn?

You will study market foundations, stock analysis, risk management, and practical decision-making for equities.

Ready to start?

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis for the current cohort.
